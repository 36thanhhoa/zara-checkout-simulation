03 / Methodology & limitations

Clearance estimate = waiting customers × mean transaction duration ÷ active counters. Theoretical hourly capacity = 60 × active counters ÷ mean transaction duration. Improvement = (current estimate − proposed estimate) ÷ current estimate × 100% (zero when the queue is empty).

This is a simplified fluid workload model, not a prediction of individual waiting time. It assumes equal counter productivity, no new arrivals, negligible setup time and excludes customers already in service. The 10-minute alert threshold and input ranges are illustrative, not verified store policies or installed capacity. Actual deployment requires validated measurements, available resources and privacy safeguards. No cameras or POS systems are connected to this prototype.
